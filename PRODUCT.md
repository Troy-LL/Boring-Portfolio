Decide whether Troy is worth a conversation. See what he builds, whether it is real, and how to reach him.

## Mode

Experience. The page is a paper calling card. The work leads. Chrome stays quiet.

## Constraints

- Brand SoT: `../personal/docs/branding.md`

- No OS chrome, gallery, Calendly embeds, skill chips, or card grids
- Booking is `/talk` → Google Appointment schedule link, new tab
- Featured work: Pupsync, EditLayer, OpenReach. seeking stays on /work.

## Success
